EN NL

Paper scoresheet for sjoelen (sjoelbak). Four gates 2-3-4-1. Thirty discs. Tap per gate. Optional volle-bak bonus of 20. Next round, undo, reset.

Not a 3D game. Not American shuffleboard. Local on the phone — no account, no tracking, no network.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
